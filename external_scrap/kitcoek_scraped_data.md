[P] Established in 1983 by a cadre of visionary industrialists, seasoned professionals, and esteemed educationalists, the Kolhapur Institute of Technology’s College of Engineering Kolhapur(Empowered Autonomous) stands as a premier institution in the realm of Engineering and Management education. Pioneering as the first self-financed engineering college in Maharashtra, the institute boasts a distinguished identity shaped by the extensive exposure and wisdom of its founders. Elevating its stature, the institute has been honoured with an "A+ Grade Status with CGPA 3.33" by The National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC), Bengaluru. Furthermore, it has attained accreditation for six departments—Civil Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Bio Technology Engineering from the prestigious National Board of Accreditation (NBA), New Delhi, a governmental body renowned for accrediting programs in institutes.Notably, the institute has secured Permanent Affiliation from Shivaji University Kolhapur and proudly holds Autonomous status. Undeniably committed to excellence, the institution has shepherded more than 15,000 engineers to success, seamlessly bridging the gap between academia and industry through campus placements and fostering a legion of entrepreneurs. In Western Maharashtra, it stands tall as a beacon of prominence and success in the realm of Engineering Education, diligently delivering optimal results for its students and stakeholders.

ABOUT US
Established in 1983 by a cadre of visionary industrialists, seasoned professionals, and esteemed educationalists, the Kolhapur Institute of Technology’s College of Engineering Kolhapur(Empowered Autonomous) stands as a premier institution in the realm of Engineering and Management education. Pioneering as the first self-financed engineering college in Maharashtra, the institute boasts a distinguished identity shaped by the extensive exposure and wisdom of its founders. Elevating its stature, the institute has been honoured with an "A+ Grade Status with CGPA 3.33" by The National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC), Bengaluru. Furthermore, it has attained accreditation for six departments—Civil Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Bio Technology Engineering from the prestigious National Board of Accreditation (NBA), New Delhi, a governmental body renowned for accrediting programs in institutes.
Notably, the institute has secured Permanent Affiliation from Shivaji University Kolhapur and proudly holds Autonomous status. Undeniably committed to excellence, the institution has shepherded more than 15,000 engineers to success, seamlessly bridging the gap between academia and industry through campus placements and fostering a legion of entrepreneurs. In Western Maharashtra, it stands tall as a beacon of prominence and success in the realm of Engineering Education, diligently delivering optimal results for its students and stakeholders.

In pursuance of the National Action Plan of the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC), Bangalore, for performance evaluation, assessment and accreditation and quality up gradation of institutions of higher education, the NAAC proposes that every accredited institution should establish an Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) as a post-accreditation quality sustenance measure. Since quality enhancement is a continuous process, the IQAC will become a part of an institution’s system and work towards realizing the goals of quality enhancement and sustenance. The prime task of the IQAC is to develop a system for conscious, consistent and catalytic improvement in the performance of institutions. The IQAC will make a significant and meaningful contribution in the post-accreditation phase of institutions. During the post-accreditation period, the IQAC will channelise the efforts and measures of an institution towards academic excellence. The guidelines provided in the following pages will facilitate the institution in the creation and operation of the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC). The work of the IQAC is the first step towards the internalization and institutionalization of quality enhancement. Its success depends upon the sense of belongingness and participation it can inculcate in all the constituents of the institution. It will not be yet another hierarchical structure or recordkeeping exercise in the institution; it will be a facilitative and participative voluntary system/unit/organ of the institution. The IQAC has the potential to become a vehicle for ushering in quality by working out intervention strategies to remove deficiencies and enhance quality. Quality circles in industries operate on similar lines.

[P] Department of Biotechnology, KIT’s College of Engineering (Autonomous), Kolhapur. Established in 2002, the Department of Biotechnology at KIT’s College of Engineering (Autonomous), Kolhapur, is among the pioneering biotechnology programs in Maharashtra. The department offers B.Tech. in Biotechnology and M.Tech. in Biochemical Engineering and Biotechnology, providing a strong foundation in biotechnology, biopharmaceuticals, enzyme technology, fermentation processes, and advanced research. The curriculum is designed to blend scientific knowledge with practical and industrial applications, preparing students for careers in industry, research, and higher education.

[P] Biotechnology has emerged as one of the fastest-growing sectors globally, driven by advances in healthcare, agriculture, environmental sustainability, and artificial intelligence. India’s biotechnology industry has grown significantly, creating vast opportunities in areas such as biomanufacturing, precision medicine, drug discovery, and bioinformatics. The department equips students with the knowledge and skills required to thrive in this evolving landscape through quality education, hands-on laboratory training, and exposure to emerging technologies. Over the years, the department has graduated more than 750 biotechnology engineers, with over 100 alumni pursuing higher studies at prestigious international universities. Graduates have secured positions in leading organizations such as Pfizer, Amgen, Biocon, and Dr. Reddy’s. Supported by strong industry collaborations, internships, research opportunities, and a dedicated alumni network, the department maintains an excellent record in placements and higher education. Student development is further enhanced through career guidance, professional certification programs, technical activities, and entrepreneurship initiatives, making KIT Biotechnology a preferred destination for aspiring biotechnology professionals.

[P] Eligibility criteria:Candidates must have studied PCM / PCB / PCMB. The minimum marks required are 45% for Open category and 40% for Reserved/EWS/PwD categories. Applicants must have a non-zero score in MHT-CET (PCM/PCB), JEE Main, or NEET.

Welcome to the Preface of the Dean Examinations & Evaluation (Controller of Examination)! As the Dean E&E, of KIT’s College of Engineering, Kolhapur (Affiliated to Shivaji University, Kolhapur), visualize the need for radical reforms in traditional examination and assessment system and are working towards evolving a credible, valid, effective and transparent evaluation system. We have set ourselves for adopting technology to facilitate easy interface for exchange of information. Education is a fundamental pillar of any society, and examinations play a significant role in assessing knowledge, skills, and aptitude. Therefore, it becomes imperative to have a well-structured and efficient examination system to evaluate students accurately.
The role of a Dean E&E is multifaceted and requires a deep understanding of educational policies, assessment methodologies, and administration. This position involves various responsibilities, including designing examination schedules, coordinating examination centers, managing examination staff, overseeing question paper setting and evaluation processes, and ensuring the timely announcement of results.
One of the primary goals of a Dean E&E is to maintain transparency, fairness, and confidentiality throughout the examination process. This involves implementing strict security measures to prevent any malpractices or incidents that may compromise the credibility of the exams. Additionally, the Dean E&E works closely with faculty, administrators, and other stakeholders to ensure that assessment methods align with the learning objectives and educational standards of the institution. Continuous improvement is an essential aspect of the Dean E&E responsibilities. Monitoring and analyzing examination data, evaluating the effectiveness of assessment methods, and implementing necessary changes based on feedback and emerging trends are crucial to enhance the overall examination process.
In today's technologically advanced world, the Dean E&E should also embrace digital solutions to streamline examination processes, such as online registration, digital evaluation, automated result generation and secure storage of examination data.
This Preface aims to provide an overview of the Dean E&E role and the importance it holds in ensuring the credibility and reliability of the examination system. It is a position that requires strong organizational skills, attention to detail, expertise in educational assessment, and a commitment to integrity. I hope that this Preface provides you with valuable insights into the responsibilities and significance of the Dean E&E in the realm of education. As you delve deeper into this subject, you will uncover the intricacies and challenges associated with this vital role.
